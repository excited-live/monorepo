## Getting started with the app

1. **Add your accounts.** Start with your personal account, then add each business or
   side project. Your day job can sit on your personal account as a salary with a
   payday.
2. **Log bills and gear.** Add recurring bills (weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly) and
   the devices you own. The app turns them into a monthly number for you.
3. **Read your runway.** The home screen shows your consolidated wealth and every
   account. Open an account to see its runway, burn, and whether it is default alive.
4. **Move money between accounts.** Putting personal money into a business is a capital
   transfer, so both runways update.

Signing in is optional. Your data lives on your phone first; signing in adds cloud
sync across devices.

## What the numbers mean

**Runway.** How many months your cash lasts at your current monthly net burn.

**Burn.** What you spend each month: recurring bills plus the monthly cost of your gear.
You can switch between a *smoothed* view (yearly bills spread across 12 months) and a
*cash* view (only bills that actually renew this month).

**Default alive.** Your income covers your burn, so the runway is not shrinking.

**Gear depreciation.** A device is spread evenly over its useful life. A laptop that
costs ฿60,000 and lasts 4 years shows as ฿1,250 a month, and drops to zero once it is
fully depreciated.

**UNTIL.** On a day-job account, the days until your next payday and the months until
your freedom buffer.

## The web planner

The web planner is a separate, optional product. It simulates your whole life over 50
years, including Thai tax and thousands of market scenarios, so you see a range of
outcomes instead of one number.

**Early access.** The planner is part of the early-access build. Nothing is charged
today, and the app itself stays free.

**Your data stays yours.** Your saved plan is never deleted, and you can export your
data at any time.

**Taxes.** Taxes are calculated by a dedicated engine using the rules for your
jurisdiction and tax year, currently **Thailand, 2026**. Results are estimates, not
guarantees. See the [Terms of Use](/docs/terms).

## Troubleshooting

**My runway looks too short.** Check that your income is added to the right account.
A salary on your personal account does not count towards a business runway.

**A yearly bill is missing from this month.** In the cash view only bills that renew
this month are counted. Switch to the smoothed view to see the monthly average.

**I did not get my sign-in email.** Check your spam folder, then request a new link.
Give it a minute before trying again.
