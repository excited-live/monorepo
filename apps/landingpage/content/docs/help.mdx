Need a hand? This page covers the questions we get most. If you cannot find your
answer here, [email support](#contact-support) and we will get back to you.

## Getting started

1. **Sign in.** Create an account with your email — we send a one-time sign-in link,
   so there is no password to remember.
2. **Tell us about your life.** The onboarding wizard asks about income, expenses,
   goals, and your family. You can change any answer later.
3. **Read your life-story chart.** excited.live simulates the years ahead and shows a
   range of outcomes, not a single number. The middle line is the most likely path;
   the shaded band shows best and worst cases.
4. **Try what-if changes.** Move a slider, toggle a scenario, or ask the assistant a
   question — the simulation updates instantly and nothing is saved until you accept it.

## Your account

**Signing in.** Enter your email and open the link we send. The link expires after a
short time; request a new one if it has gone stale.

**Saved plans.** When you are signed in, your plan is saved to your account so you can
come back to it on any device. Everything you enter stays private — see the
[Privacy Policy](/docs/privacy).

**Signing out.** Use the account menu in the top-right corner. Your plans stay saved.

## Plans and simulation

**How the simulation works.** The engine takes your income, expenses, inflation, and
goals, then projects them forward year by year. It also runs thousands of market
scenarios to show how results could vary. Results are estimates, not guarantees —
the assumptions are listed in the app and in the [Terms of Use](/docs/terms).

**Editing a plan.** Add or change rows directly, or ask the assistant for a proposal.
Proposals are shown as suggestions: nothing changes until you accept or reject them.

**Scenario toggles and sliders.** Toggles switch a situation on and off (for example,
a career break). Sliders adjust an amount across a range so you can see the effect
before committing.

## Taxes

Taxes are calculated by a dedicated engine using the rules for your jurisdiction and
tax year — currently **Thailand, 2026**. The app automatically applies deductions and
shows how tax-saving options (such as ThaiESG/RMF contributions) affect your results.
Other jurisdictions are on the roadmap.

## Subscription and trial

excited.live is a paid product with a **7-day free trial** for new accounts. Pricing,
the trial, and the current billing period are always shown in the app under
**Settings → Billing**.

**After the trial.** When the trial ends you need an active subscription to keep using
the app. Your saved plans are not deleted.

**Managing or cancelling.** You can review or change your plan any time in
**Settings → Billing**.

## Troubleshooting

**The chart looks wrong.** Check that your income and expense periods cover the years
you expect. A missing year can flatten part of the chart.

**I did not get my sign-in email.** Check your spam folder, then request a new link.
Give it a minute before trying again.

**A number changed after I reloaded.** Unaccepted proposals and unsaved edits are
discarded on reload — accept the change first if you want to keep it.
