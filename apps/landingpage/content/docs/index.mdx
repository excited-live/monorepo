## What is excited.live?

excited.live is a **free mobile app that shows how long your cash lasts**. It keeps your
personal money, your day job and every business or side project in one place, so you can
see your runway, your monthly burn, and what you can safely spend.

When tracking feels easy, the same numbers feed our **web planner**, a 50-year life
simulation with Thai tax and what-if sliders.

## Where do the numbers come from?

The calculation engines are open source — see the
[GitHub repository](https://github.com/fromsukong/excited.live). Burn, depreciation and
tax rules live in dedicated, tested packages, so the maths never depends on an AI guess.
