## What is excited.live?

excited.live is free during early access. Your account holds your money picture — personal, day job and side projects — and your own AI agent can connect over MCP to keep it up to date. The Android app is the human door: it shows how long your cash lasts.

## Connect your agent

Point your agent at `https://api.excited.live/mcp` and it can read your runway and keep it up to date. MCP sign-in is in final testing until launch, so expect the occasional step to move.

Set up Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Claude or ChatGPT in the [connect guide](/docs/mcp/).

## When does it launch?

excited.live launches on 31 Oct 2026. Create an account at the [sign-in page](https://auth.excited.live/sign-in?mode=signup), or read [Connect your agent](/docs/mcp/) if you are bringing your own agent.

## Not in early access yet

The 50-year web planner (accounts, bills, gear depreciation and runway). We will announce it when it ships.

## Where do the numbers come from?

The calculation engines are open source — see the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/fromsukong/excited.live). Burn, depreciation and tax rules live in dedicated, tested packages, so the maths never depends on an AI guess.
