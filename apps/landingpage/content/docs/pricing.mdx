excited.live is **free during early access**. No payment is taken today, and you do
not need a card to sign up.

## What you get

- Your excited.live account and workspace settings.
- The agent connection endpoint (MCP), so your own AI agent can connect. MCP sign-in is
  in final testing during early access. excited.live launches on 31 Oct 2026.

## Not in early access yet

- The 50-year web planner (accounts, bills, gear depreciation and runway). We will
  announce it when it ships.
