excited.live is **free during launch**. No card is required, and nothing is charged today. There is no trial end date, no automatic charge, and no plan, seat or usage fee.

Agents can read the same answer as plain text at [`/pricing.md`](https://excited.live/pricing.md).

## What you get

- Your excited.live account and workspace settings.
- The agent connection endpoint (MCP), so your own AI agent can connect. MCP sign-in is in final testing during early access. excited.live launches on 31 Oct 2026.

## Not in early access yet

- The 50-year web planner (accounts, bills, gear depreciation and runway). We will announce it when it ships.
